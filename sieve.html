The method

  1. List every whole number from 2 to n. The number 1 is left out because it is not prime.
  2. Let p = 2, the first prime.
  3. Cross out the multiples of p, starting at p² and counting up in steps of p.
  4. Move p to the next number that is still unmarked. It is prime, because no smaller prime divides it.
  5. Repeat while p² ≤ n. Once p² is larger than n, stop: every number still unmarked is prime.
The rule
Cross out multiples of each prime p:
start at p × p
— and —
stop once p × p > n
Any multiple smaller than p², such as 3 × p, has a smaller prime factor and was crossed out earlier. Every composite number up to n has a prime factor no larger than √n.
